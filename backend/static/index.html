SIH 2026 Problem ID 26066

Subsurface ocean temperature is vital for upper-ocean heat content, marine heatwaves, and monsoons. In-situ ARGO measurements are spatially sparse.

OceanEmbed transforms 7 surface satellite observation channels (SST, SSS, SSH, OSCAR Currents, CCMP Winds) into compact 256-D latent ocean embeddings, predicting the complete 3D vertical temperature profile across 15 standard depth levels (0m to 1000m) over the North Indian Ocean at 0.25° daily resolution.

Target Region:5°N–30°N, 45°E–105°E
Resolution:0.25° × 0.25° (101 × 241)
Active Ocean Cells:11,742 water points
Ground Truth Reanalysis:GLORYS12V1 (50 levels)
Validation Benchmark:INCOIS Gridded ARGO LAS

Technical Note on Spatial & Temporal Alignment:

All satellite input products (OSTIA at 0.05°, SMAP at 0.125°, DUACS at 0.25°, OSCAR at 0.25°, ASCAT/CCMP 6-hourly) are regridded onto the common North Indian Ocean 0.25° × 0.25° grid [5°N–30°N, 45°E–105°E] using bilinear interpolation with land-mask preservation and 24-hour temporal averaging. CoordConv channels are injected to provide explicit spatial coordinates to the deep neural network.
